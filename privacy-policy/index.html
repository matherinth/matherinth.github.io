      Privacy Policy
      Last updated: July 2026

      This Privacy Policy describes how matherinth.github.io ("we", "our", or "us") handles user information when you use our website and authenticate via Google OAuth.

      Information We Collect: When you log in using Google OAuth, we collect and store your basic Google profile information (name, email, and profile picture) in our Cloudflare D1 database and session management via Cloudflare KV to maintain your logged-in state.

      How We Use Information: The retrieved information is used solely to authenticate and personalize your user experience on the platform.

      Data Sharing and Disclosure: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data is processed securely through Cloudflare Workers and D1.

      Data Retention: We retain your user profile data in our database until you explicitly request account deletion via the dashboard interface.

      Third-Party Services: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, uses Google OAuth for authentication, and relies on Cloudflare Workers, KV, and D1 for backend services. Please review their respective privacy policies for details on their data handling practices.

      Contact Us: If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our repository contact details.
